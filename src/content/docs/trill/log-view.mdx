The log view is where Trill queries run interactively. Everything here also
lives in the URL, so a link reopens the same search and time window.

## Search and autocomplete

Type a [Trill](/trill/) query in the search bar. Results update as you type
and immediately when you press **Enter**.

Autocomplete suggests as you type:

- **Terms:** `level>=`, `service:`, `env:`, `field.`, `in:fields`, `in:all`.
- **Values:** level names after `level:`, and the services and environments
  seen recently after `service:` and `env:`.
- **Fields:** after `field.`, the keys found in the last 24 hours of events,
  with their types. After `field.<key>:`, that field's most common values.
  Typing a bare word that names a field, like `imei`, offers `field.imei:`.

| Key              | Does                                               |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `↓` / `↑`        | Move through suggestions (`↓` also opens them)     |
| `Tab`            | Accept the highlighted suggestion, or the first    |
| `Enter`          | Accept a highlighted suggestion; otherwise search  |
| `Ctrl` + `Space` | Open suggestions                                   |
| `Esc`            | Close suggestions; press again to clear the search |

Below the bar:

- **Level chips** toggle a `level:` term.
- **Text in** sets where free text looks: **message** (default), **fields**,
  or **both**. It writes `in:fields` or `in:all` into the query.
- **Clear** (the × in the search bar) empties the search.
- **Saved searches** keep up to 8 queries in this browser. Click one to apply
  it, or its × to remove it.

## Time window

Pick a range from 5 minutes to 30 days, or enter exact start and end times.
To zoom, drag across the histogram to select a range (drag the handles to
refine it), then press **Zoom In** or Enter; clear it from the same picker. A
search window is at most 31 days.

## Live tail

**Live** streams new events over SSE as they arrive, filtered by the same
query, with no polling. **Follow** keeps the newest event in view. The optional
refresh rate in the time picker (off by default) re-queries the window every
15 seconds, 1 minute, or 5 minutes, and rescales with the window.

## Histogram

The histogram shows event volume over the window, stacked by level, for the
events that **match the query**. Click a coloured level segment in a bar to
filter to that level.

## Event drawer

Click any row to open the event: its time, level, service, env, the API key
that ingested it, its UUID v7 id (copyable), and the full message.

Its **fields** are listed one per row, nested keys flattened (`data.rss`). Hover
a field and click its filter button to add the matching `field.` term to the
search. The **raw JSON** toggle shows the original object.

<Aside type="tip" title="Usage by key">
  `key:<id>` matches events ingested by one API key. Open a key's actions in
  **Settings → Projects → manage** and choose **View usage** to apply it.
</Aside>
